FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about syllabus, notes, lab manuals, previous year papers, and other digital learning resources.

Where can I download the latest CSE syllabus?

The latest semester-wise curriculum and syllabus are available in the Curriculum section. Select your semester to download the official PDF.

01

Are previous year question papers available?

Yes. The Digital Library provides previous year semester examination papers for Computer Science & Engineering to help students prepare effectively.

02

Can I download notes and lab manuals?

Yes. Students can access subject-wise lecture notes, practical lab manuals, cheat sheets, and reference materials in PDF format.

03

Which semesters are covered in the Digital Library?

Resources are organized semester-wise for all six diploma semesters, including syllabus, notes, previous year papers, lab manuals, magazines, and other academic materials.
